## Introduction

A luminous formation photographed over [Hamburg]({{ 'why-hamburgs-ufo-mysteries-usually-fade/' | relative_url }}) in early March 2024 briefly attracted UFO speculation, partly because its bright, separated streaks looked unlike an ordinary aircraft. A Falcon 9 launch from Florida was soon proposed as a possible source, while later local reporting described the object instead as an LED-lit stunt kite. The difficulty is that the surviving public record does not provide enough basic evidence to test either identification properly.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Hamburg_155277_march_2024_lights_36e42c-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for March 2024" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

No widely accessible report supplies the original photographs at full resolution alongside an exact observation time, viewing direction, duration and camera location. Nor is there a published trajectory comparison tying the display to a particular rocket stage or satellite pass. The kite explanation is plausible and may be correct, but the available reporting does not show how it was verified.

## What circulated online

The incident appears to have begun with images of an unusually arranged set of lights over Hamburg. Contemporary descriptions referred to a bright object producing blinking or luminous streaks against the night sky. Their apparent spacing encouraged viewers to see a formation rather than a single point source, opening the familiar online debate between spacecraft, drones, illuminated kites and something genuinely unidentified.

A later article in the *Hamburger Abendblatt*, published on 4 June 2024, recalled the excitement and explicitly mentioned two early SpaceX theories: debris from a recently launched Falcon 9 rocket, or one of the satellites it had deployed. The same article then dismissed those ideas and stated that the supposed UFO had proved to be a manoeuvrable kite fitted with LED lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abendblatt.de/hamburg/bergedorf/article242486986/Warum-Ausserirdische-nichts-von-uns-wissen-wollen.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abendblatt.de">[Abendblatt]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abendblatt.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Warum Außerirdische nichts von uns wissen wollen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Warum Außerirdische nichts von uns wissen wollenJune 4, 2024 — 4 Jun 2024 — Anfang März dieses Jahres sorgte ein leuchtendes Ob...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 4, 2024</span></span></span>

That retrospective account is the strongest accessible local claim about the outcome, but it leaves important questions unanswered. It does not, in the material available through web indexing, identify the kite operator, say where it was flown, explain whether police or journalists saw it on the ground, or show a comparison between the kite’s lighting pattern and the circulated images. A conclusion without its supporting chain of evidence is less useful than a documented identification.

There is also a risk of historical confusion. Hamburg had a well-reported illuminated-kite scare on 5 March 2013, when concerned callers reported a UFO over the Heiligengeistfeld. Police attended and found a man flying a kite equipped with electric lights; he brought it down after officers spoke to him. That older incident has a clear date, location, police response and identified operator.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bild.de/regional/hamburg/ufo-ueber-hamburg-war-nur-beleuchteter-drache-29390764.bild.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bild.de">[BILD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bild.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dies sei zwar erlaubt, sagte ein Polizeisprecher, der Mann</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BILD«Ufo» über Hamburg war nur beleuchteter Drache &#124; RegionalMarch 6, 2013 — 6 Mar 2013 — Ein Mann hatte einen mit elektrischen Lampen be...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 6, 2013</span></span></span> The similarity makes an LED kite entirely credible in 2024, but it also means later summaries need to distinguish carefully between a new event and a familiar explanation drawn from Hamburg’s earlier UFO history.

## Why a Falcon 9 link was considered

The rocket theory was not invented from nothing. SpaceX launched a Falcon 9 carrying 23 [Starlink]({{ 'starlink-667de7/' | relative_url }}) satellites from Cape Canaveral on 4 March 2024. The mission, Starlink 6-41, lifted off at 6.54 pm Eastern Standard Time, or 23.54 UTC.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://spaceflightnow.com/2024/03/04/live-coverage-spacex-to-launch-falcon-9-rocket-from-cape-canaveral-with-23-starlink-satellites-3/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: spaceflightnow.com">[Spaceflight Now]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">spaceflightnow.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Starlink 6-41 mission on Monday,</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Spaceflight NowSpaceX launched Falcon 9 rocket from Cape Canaveral...4 Mar 2024 — SpaceX launched Falcon 9 rocket from Cape Canaveral wi...</span></span></span> Another Falcon 9 had launched the Crew-8 mission from Florida shortly beforehand, on the night of 3 March local time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/space/spacex-launches-its-eighth-long-duration-crew-orbit-nasa-2024-03-04/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Space X launches eighth long-duration crew for International Space Station orbit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The crew includes three U.S. astronauts—flight commander Matthew Dominick, mission pilot Dr. Michael Barratt, and aerospace engineer Jean...</span></span></span> With several space launches occurring close to the reported Hamburg observation window, checking orbital activity was a sensible first step.

Rocket missions can create several visually confusing effects. A newly deployed group of satellites may appear as a line of separate moving lights. A rocket’s upper stage can produce a diffuse cloud when it vents remaining propellant. Under suitable illumination, exhaust or vented material high above the dark side of Earth can reflect sunlight and appear much brighter and larger than the hardware producing it. These effects are now frequent sources of UFO reports.

Starlink spacecraft can also be conspicuous while raising their orbits. Research published in 2024 found that Starlink V2 Mini satellites below an altitude of 357 kilometres were markedly brighter than those at higher operating altitudes. The authors noted that low-orbit spacecraft attract considerable public attention and have even been reported by airline pilots as unidentified aerial phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12007" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">arXiv The Brightness of Starlink Mini Satellites During Orbit-Raising</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Brightness of Starlink Mini Satellites During Orbit-RaisingMay 20, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 20, 2024</span></span></span>

The proposed Falcon 9 connection nevertheless required more than a nearby launch date. Investigators would have needed to establish that the relevant rocket stage or freshly released satellite group was above Hamburg’s horizon at the observation time, moving in the correct direction and illuminated by the Sun. A launch in Florida cannot be linked to a German sighting merely because both occurred during the same week.

The appearance also matters. Fresh Starlink deployments normally resemble a steadily travelling “train” of points rather than blinking streaks holding a compact pattern. Rocket exhaust tends to form a plume, arc, expanding cloud or spiral. A formation that repeatedly changed shape, remained over one area or traced angular lines would fit a tethered LED kite or coordinated drones more naturally. Without the original sequence and timing, however, those distinctions cannot be applied confidently.

## The missing evidence

The case remains weakly documented because the information needed for reconstruction was not preserved, or at least was not published. Three gaps are particularly important.

**No precise observation record.** The public summaries do not provide a reliable time to the minute, an exact camera position or the direction in which the witness was looking. Those details would permit a check against satellite-tracking data, aircraft routes and astronomical objects. “Early March over Hamburg” is too broad for orbital identification.

**No documented motion sequence.** A still photograph compresses several possibilities into one image. Long exposure, camera movement and digital night modes can turn a single blinking light into separated dashes or repeated streaks. A video would reveal whether the lights travelled smoothly across the sky, circled a fixed point, changed formation or remained tethered to one area.

**No transparent verification of the kite claim.** The later local report presents the LED kite as the answer, but the accessible account does not disclose who confirmed it or on what evidence. ## A partly solved Hamburg case

The available evidence weighs against an exotic interpretation. There is no report of radar confirmation, aviation disruption, physical debris or an independently observed craft. The strongest candidates are ordinary technologies capable of creating extraordinary-looking images: an illuminated kite, a rocket-related effect, satellites, drones or a camera artefact.

Among them, the LED-kite explanation has the advantage of later local reporting and a close Hamburg precedent. The Falcon 9 proposal has the advantage of a real launch on 4 March and the well-established ability of rockets and newly deployed satellites to generate UFO reports. Neither, however, is accompanied in the accessible record by the measurements needed for a conclusive match.

This distinction is important within Hamburg’s wider UFO history. A case can be probably conventional without being fully solved. “Unresolved” need not mean that all explanations are equally likely; it may simply mean that poor documentation prevents a final identification. The March 2024 formation belongs in that middle category: not strong evidence of an unknown craft, but also not a model example of transparent [investigation]({{ 'investigation/' | relative_url }}).

Later reporting weakened the original UFO speculation by offering a mundane explanation. It did not completely close the case because it supplied the conclusion more clearly than the proof. Hamburg’s 2024 lights are therefore best remembered as a lesson in evidential risk: once striking photographs circulate without complete time, location and motion data, even a likely explanation can remain harder to demonstrate than it should be.

    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
