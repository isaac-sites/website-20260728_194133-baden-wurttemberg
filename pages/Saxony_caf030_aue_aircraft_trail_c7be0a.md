## Introduction

The Aue sighting of 28 January 2013 is best understood as a sunlit aircraft contrail seen from a misleading angle, not as evidence of an unknown vehicle. Early that morning, a local woman photographed a bright, curved trail near the rising Sun and described it as appearing suddenly, bending and then climbing steeply out of view. The images attracted regional press coverage and became one of the Erzgebirge’s more memorable modern UFO scares. Yet the surviving account contains no radar confirmation, independent measurement or identified craft performing unusual manoeuvres. A German UFO investigator who examined the photographs instead favoured an aircraft condensation trail.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aue-badschlema.de/de/datei/anzeigen/id/4318%2C33/chronik_2013.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aue-badschlema.de">[Aue-Bad Schlema]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aue-badschlema.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">chronik 2013</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aue-Bad SchlemaChronik 2013...</span></span></span>

The case matters within [Saxony]({{ 'why-saxonys-ufo-history-remains-so/' | relative_url }})’s UFO history because it shows how an ordinary high-altitude flight can look extraordinary when three effects coincide: low sunlight makes the trail glow, perspective disguises a level flight as a steep ascent, and changing atmospheric conditions cause the visible trail to begin or end abruptly.

## What the witness photographed

Kathleen Schneidenbach reported seeing the luminous trail over Aue shortly after daybreak on 28 January 2013. Aue’s municipal chronicle, summarising the contemporary newspaper story, records that she photographed the phenomenon in the light of the rising Sun. She interpreted its unfamiliar shape dramatically, suggesting that extraterrestrials might have been involved and saying that she intended to search nearby woodland for traces of a spacecraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aue-badschlema.de/de/datei/anzeigen/id/4318%2C33/chronik_2013.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aue-badschlema.de">[Aue-Bad Schlema]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aue-badschlema.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">chronik 2013</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aue-Bad SchlemaChronik 2013...</span></span></span>

Her most useful description concerned the trail’s movement rather than its supposed origin. She said it appeared “out of nowhere”, curved and then seemed to rise steeply before disappearing. Those details explain why the sighting felt unlike a routine aeroplane passing overhead. A straight contrail viewed broadside is easy to recognise; one directed partly towards or away from an observer can be compressed by perspective into a short, steep or apparently curved feature.

The photographs were passed by the regional press to UFO specialists. Hans-Werner Peiniger, chairman of the Gesellschaft zur Erforschung des UFO-Phänomens, did not claim that the pictures showed an exotic craft. His stated assessment was that they probably depicted an aircraft contrail. The organisation’s surviving media archive reproduces the episode as a press report rather than presenting it as a formally unresolved, high-evidence case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufo-forschung.de/gep-in-den-medien/ufo-alarm-ueber-dem-erzgebirge" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufo-forschung.de">[UFO Forschung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufo-forschung.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Forschung Ufo-Alarm über dem Erzgebirge!</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Gesellschaft zur Erforschung des UFO-Phänomens e.V.Januar 2013 erschien in der Chemnitzer Morgenpost ein Artikel, in dem auch die GEP E...</span></span></span>

That distinction is important. The photographs documented a real luminous feature, but a photograph of an unidentified trail is not the same thing as evidence that a structured vehicle behaved impossibly. No published account describes a visible fuselage, windows, lights arranged on a craft, physical debris or interaction with the ground. What witnesses and readers were trying to identify was primarily the glowing trail itself.

## Why an airliner and contrail fit the photographs

A contrail is a narrow ice cloud produced when warm, moist aircraft exhaust mixes with sufficiently cold air at cruising altitude. Whether one forms depends strongly on temperature and humidity; a jet can therefore produce a trail in one patch of air and none in the next. This offers a straightforward explanation for Schneidenbach’s impression that the streak began suddenly and later vanished. The aeroplane need not have appeared from nowhere: only the visible condensation did.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/learn-about/weather/types-of-weather/clouds/unusual-cloud-formations/contrails" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: weather.metoffice.gov.uk">[metoffice.gov.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">weather.metoffice.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Met Office Contrails or condensation trails</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Strictly speaking, there is only one type of contrail, however, there is a similar effect called “Wingtip vortices”.... The early-morning timing strengthens that interpretation. A high-altitude aircraft and its trail can remain illuminated by direct sunlight while the ground below is still comparatively dark. Near sunrise, the long path of sunlight through the atmosphere removes more blue light, allowing a normally white contrail to appear yellow, orange or red. NASA has documented precisely this effect in photographs of aircraft trails taken shortly after sunrise, when both the aircraft and contrail were lit from below and appeared bright red.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap231204.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apod.nasa.gov">[Astronomy Picture of the Day]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apod.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Captured early last month from Bolton, UK, the featured image was taken so soon after sunrise that the plane was sunlit from below, as wa...</span></span></span>

The apparent steep climb is also compatible with an ordinary level flight. When an aeroplane travels approximately along the observer’s line of sight, distance is compressed in the image. Its horizontal path can then appear to rise sharply from the horizon, much as parallel railway tracks seem to converge in the distance. A modest turn can similarly look like a pronounced bend because the viewer is not seeing the route from above. Technical explanations of widely publicised “missile-like” trails have shown that nearly horizontal aircraft paths can appear vertical when photographed from the ground at a shallow angle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://contrailscience.com/contrails-are-usually-horizontal/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: contrailscience.com">[Contrail Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">contrailscience.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Contrail Science Contrails are Usually Horizontal – Contrail Science</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">This 5-10 degree angle between heading and contrail appears greatly magnified by the perspective...</span></span></span>

Wind and the age of different portions of the trail can add further distortion. Fresh contrail near the aircraft is usually narrow, while older sections spread and drift as ice crystals are carried by air currents. Official aviation and environmental sources note that contrail shape and persistence vary with atmospheric conditions, wind and flight path. A trail photographed at one moment therefore need not preserve a clean geometrical record of the aircraft’s exact route.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.faa.gov/contrails" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: faa.gov">[faa.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">faa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Therefore, it may be challenging, or even impossible to correlate a specific airplane that created a contrail using flight tracking websites...</span></span></span>

Taken together, the reported features align closely with an airliner explanation:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **The bright colour:** low-angle sunrise illumination.
* **The sudden appearance:** entry into cold, humid air capable of supporting a contrail.
* **The curve:** an ordinary course change, wind distortion or perspective compression.
* **The apparent vertical ascent:** a level aircraft moving partly towards or away from the observer.
* **The disappearance:** the aircraft entering drier air, becoming too distant to resolve or leaving the illuminated viewing geometry.

</div>

None of these effects requires unusual propulsion or exceptional aircraft performance.

## What remained unconfirmed

The explanation is persuasive but not a complete reconstruction of a named flight. The publicly accessible summaries do not provide an exact observation time to the minute, the witness’s precise position and viewing direction, original photograph metadata, a measured angular path or contemporaneous air-traffic records. Without those details, it is difficult to match the trail confidently to a particular airliner or calculate its route over the Erzgebirge.

This evidential gap should not be confused with evidence against the aircraft explanation. Flight-tracking websites, especially those available in 2013, did not necessarily display every aircraft, and even modern public trackers can be incomplete. The Federal Aviation Administration also cautions that matching a specific aeroplane to a contrail can be difficult because the visible trail may persist and drift after the aircraft has moved on.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.faa.gov/contrails" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: faa.gov">[Federal Aviation Administration]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">faa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Therefore, it may be challenging, or even impossible to correlate a specific airplane that created a contrail using flight tracking websites...</span></span></span>

There is likewise no published indication that investigators recovered physical traces, obtained radar evidence of an unknown object or found independent witnesses who reported a solid craft. The surviving municipal and UFO-group accounts repeat the original witness statement and Peiniger’s contrail assessment, but they do not document a subsequent technical investigation that overturned it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aue-badschlema.de/de/datei/anzeigen/id/4318%2C33/chronik_2013.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aue-badschlema.de">[Aue-Bad Schlema]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aue-badschlema.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">chronik 2013</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aue-Bad SchlemaChronik 2013...</span></span></span>

The lack of a named aircraft leaves a minor historical uncertainty; it does not preserve a strong UFO mystery.

## A useful Saxon example of mistaken motion

Aue’s luminous trail is valuable less for what it reveals about unknown craft than for what it reveals about sky observation. Witnesses judge speed, height and direction using familiar objects as reference points. A distant aircraft against an open sky provides few such cues. When its body is too small to see and only a glowing trail remains visible, the observer may interpret changes in brightness and angle as sudden acceleration, climbing or disappearance.

That mechanism recurs in reports of supposed rockets, falling objects and vertically climbing UFOs. It is particularly effective around sunrise and sunset, when aircraft at cruising altitude may still be brilliantly illuminated above a darker landscape. The resulting streak can look fiery, self-luminous and much closer than it really is. NASA imagery and meteorological guidance show that coloured, sunlit contrails are normal products of lighting and atmospheric scattering rather than signs that an object is burning or emitting an unusual beam.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap231204.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apod.nasa.gov">[nasa.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apod.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Astronomy Picture of the Day</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Captured early last month from Bolton, UK, the featured image was taken so soon after sunrise that the plane was sunlit from below, as wa...</span></span></span>

    return encodeURIComponent(String(value || '').replace(/\s+/g, ' ').trim()).replace(/%20/g, '+');
  }
  function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
