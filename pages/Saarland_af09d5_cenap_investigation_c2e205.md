## Introduction

CENAP plays a practical but easily misunderstood role in [Saarland]({{ 'saarlands-ufo-story-between-mystery-and/' | relative_url }})’s modern UFO history. It is not a government investigation office, an aviation authority or a scientific institute. It is a small, privately run reporting network that receives witness accounts, checks them against ordinary astronomical and technological causes, and tells observers whether their sighting can be identified. In a state with relatively few reports, that screening work supplies much of the publicly accessible evidence about what people are actually seeing.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Saarland_af09d5_cenap_investigation_c2e205-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for CENAP" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

The clearest recent example is 2024. Five Saarland enquiries reached CENAP. Four were specifically identified as two laser-light effects, a night parachutist and an unusually bright star. The fifth was not described in comparable public detail. Rather than revealing a local wave of extraordinary craft, the cases show how apparently strange reports often become understandable once the time, direction and circumstances are reconstructed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sr.de/sr/home/nachrichten/panorama/ufo-meldungen_im_saarland_100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sr.de">[SR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">S R.de: Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr...</span></span></span>

## How reports are submitted

CENAP’s investigation begins with the witness rather than with instruments or official surveillance. People contact the network after seeing a light, object or movement that they cannot immediately explain. The reporting service is advertised as available around the clock, although in practice it is operated by a very small number of volunteers rather than by a staffed public agency.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sr.de/sr/home/nachrichten/panorama/ufo-meldungen_im_saarland_100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sr.de">[sr.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">S R.de: Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr...</span></span></span>

CENAP also encourages [witnesses]({{ 'witnesses-b354a2/' | relative_url }}) who are unsure of the compass direction to name nearby towns, buildings or other landmarks lying beneath the object. This can allow an investigator to reconstruct the line of sight even when the observer cannot estimate azimuth, altitude or distance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hjkc.de/25.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hjkc.de">[CENAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hjkc.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">CENAPRaumfahrt+Astronomie-Blog von CENAP</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Über CENAP...</span></span></span>

These details matter more than an elaborate description of the object’s supposed shape. A bright point reported as “stationary in the south-west at 7.15 pm”, for example, can be checked against the positions of planets and stars. A row of lights moving from west to east can be compared with satellite passages. Without a reliable time and location, even a striking photograph may be of limited value because the investigator cannot test which known objects occupied that part of the sky.

CENAP co-founder Hansjürgen Köhler has said that the combination of date, time and viewing direction makes identification possible in most cases. The network aims to resolve reports quickly, sometimes within 24 hours, while memories are fresh and relevant flight or astronomical information is still easy to retrieve.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sr.de/sr/home/nachrichten/panorama/ufo-meldungen_im_saarland_100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sr.de">[SR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">S R.de: Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr...</span></span></span>

## Which checks investigators perform

CENAP’s method is essentially one of elimination. Investigators first ask whether a reported object matches something known to have been visible from the witness’s position. They do not begin by trying to prove or disprove an extraterrestrial explanation. Instead, they test [common causes]({{ 'common-causes-7b27b0/' | relative_url }}) in an order shaped by the report’s appearance, movement and duration.

### Astronomical objects

For night-time cases, CENAP checks the sky at the reported place and time. Bright planets such as Venus and Jupiter, prominent stars such as Sirius, meteors and unusual alignments can all appear unfamiliar to observers who do not regularly watch the sky. Atmospheric turbulence may make a star seem to flash in different colours or move slightly, particularly when it is low above the horizon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell-radio/ufo-meldestelle-cenap-in-den-meisten-faellen-sind-es-drohnen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO-Meldestelle CENAP: &quot;In den meisten Fällen sind es Drohnen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO-Meldestelle CENAP: &quot;In den meisten Fällen sind es Drohnen&quot;...</span></span></span>

This category is directly relevant to Saarland. One of the four specifically identified CENAP reports from 2024 was an unusually bright star. The witness was not necessarily mistaken about seeing something conspicuous; the error lay in interpreting a real astronomical object as an unknown craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sr.de/sr/home/nachrichten/panorama/ufo-meldungen_im_saarland_100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sr.de">[SR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">S R.de: Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr...</span></span></span>

### Satellites and space activity

Investigators can compare reports with predicted passes of satellites, the International Space Station, rocket stages and other objects in orbit. Starlink satellites have become especially important because newly launched groups may appear as a neat procession of lights. To someone encountering the formation for the first time, it can look coordinated, artificial and unlike a conventional aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zdfheute.de/panorama/ufo-sichtungen-rekord-cenap-forschungsgruppe-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zdfheute.de">[ZDFheute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zdfheute.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo sichtungen rekord cenap forschungsgruppe 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rekord an UFO-Sichtungen: 2024 mehr als 1.000 Meldungen...</span></span></span>

Rocket activity can produce even stranger effects. Fuel releases, re-entry trails and burning debris may create glowing clouds, spirals or long streaks visible across several regions. Such events often generate many reports at once, allowing investigators to recognise a shared cause from the timing and geographical spread.

A February 2025 rocket-debris event produced a surge of calls to CENAP from elsewhere in south-western Germany. Saarland contributed no calls on that occasion, apparently because the phenomenon was most visible farther north. ### Lasers, drones and event lighting

Two of Saarland’s five 2024 reports were traced to laser-light effect equipment. Lasers and powerful event lights become especially conspicuous when beams strike low cloud, haze or moisture. A projected patch may appear to move rapidly across the cloud base, reverse direction or vanish abruptly. Because the illuminated surface is distant and often invisible, the observer may perceive the light as a solid object moving through open sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sr.de/sr/home/nachrichten/panorama/ufo-meldungen_im_saarland_100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sr.de">[SR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">S R.de: Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr...</span></span></span>

### Photographic and video artefacts

Images are helpful only when interpreted carefully. Smartphone cameras automatically adjust exposure, focus, sharpening and noise reduction. These processes may enlarge a point of light, alter its shape or produce apparent structures that were not visible to the eye.

CENAP compares submitted photographs and videos with examples in its archive. Nationally, reported “objects” in images have proved to be lens reflections, insects or birds photographed close to the camera and rendered as blurred shapes. Saarland’s Peterberg Observatory has independently noted that automatic camera settings and filters can distort lighting conditions and create misleading impressions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sr.de/sr/home/nachrichten/panorama/ufo-meldungen_im_saarland_100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sr.de">[SR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">S R.de: Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr...</span></span></span>

The presence of a photograph therefore does not automatically strengthen a sighting. An image may be useful when it preserves direction, movement or a recognisable light pattern, but weak when it contains only an enlarged, unfocused dot with no horizon or reference point.

## How conclusions are classified

For the public, the most important distinction is between a report that has been explained and one that remains [unidentified]({{ 'unidentified/' | relative_url }}) because the information is incomplete. Those two outcomes are often confused.

An **identified report** is one for which the reported time, position, appearance and movement agree sufficiently with a known object or event. The Saarland laser effects, night parachutist and bright star belong in this category. Identification does not mean that investigators reproduced every detail perfectly; it means that an ordinary source provides a coherent match without requiring an extraordinary explanation.

An **insufficiently documented report** cannot be tested properly because essential information is absent or contradictory. CENAP has said that its unresolved national cases commonly lack basics such as an exact date, time or location. In such circumstances, “unresolved” is a statement about the quality of the evidence, not about the nature of the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thestar.com.my/lifestyle/living/2025/01/06/decades-of-debunking-one-man039s-quest-to-explain-ufos-without-aliens" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thestar.com.my">[The Star]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thestar.com.my</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The StarDecades of debunking: One man&#x27;s quest to explain UFOs...January 6, 2025 — 6 Jan 2025 — For all those immediately thinking of ali...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 6, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why CENAP matters in Saarland

CENAP’s importance comes partly from an institutional gap. Germany does not have one publicly accessible national state agency that receives all civilian UFO reports and publishes a complete, standardised catalogue. Modern German case material is instead divided among private organisations, individual investigators, observatories, media reports and occasional official records. A 2024 review of German UAP research described decades of data collection by private research groups and emphasised that this work remains little known outside the country.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://zenodo.org/records/10579210" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zenodo.org">[Zenodo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zenodo.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">cal development of basic definitions of “UAP</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Research in Germany: Single Case Studies, Data...29 Jan 2024 — This paper presents the current state of knowledge and lists ke...</span></span></span>

For Saarland, a small state with a low number of reports, this means CENAP can strongly influence the surviving public record. Five reports sent to one private network do not represent every unusual sighting made in the state. Some witnesses contact observatories, police, newspapers or other UFO organisations; many tell nobody. Peterberg Observatory, for example, received three Saarland enquiries in 2024 and reported that most such phenomena were readily explicable as meteors, satellites, aircraft, balloons or imaging errors.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sr.de/sr/home/nachrichten/panorama/ufo-meldungen_im_saarland_100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sr.de">[SR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">S R.de: Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fünf UFO-Sichtungen im Saarland vergangenes Jahr...</span></span></span>

CENAP’s annual figures should therefore be read as reports received, not as a complete census of everything observed over Saarland. They are affected by publicity, awareness of the hotline, weather, major astronomical events and whether witnesses decide to seek an explanation.

2. **Pattern recognition.** A long-running archive helps investigators recognise repeated appearances of stars, satellites, balloons, lasers and camera artefacts.

## The limits of a private investigation

CENAP’s conclusions should not be treated as official determinations. The network describes itself as astronomically oriented and openly sceptical, with the stated aim of tracing sightings to their causes. Its investigations are generally conducted through witness interviews, database checks, mapping and image comparison rather than through a permanent sensor network or statutory access to military radar and classified aviation data.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hjkc.de/25.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hjkc.de">[CENAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hjkc.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">CENAPRaumfahrt+Astronomie-Blog von CENAP</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Über CENAP...</span></span></span>

This approach is effective for common misidentifications but has limits. Witness estimates of distance and speed are often unreliable. Public flight-tracking services may omit some military, police or private activity. A photograph can be too poor to analyse. Investigators may also lack independent witnesses or original files.

CENAP’s strongly sceptical identity is relevant when assessing its work. Scepticism is useful because most reports do have ordinary causes, and investigators need familiarity with repeated false alarms. However, a predisposition towards explanation can become a weakness if a proposed match is accepted without enough case-specific evidence. CENAP has faced criticism within German UFO circles over methodology, its treatment of witnesses and an alleged tendency to investigate mainly from a desk or by telephone. Those criticisms do not prove that its Saarland identifications are wrong, but they underline the need to distinguish a plausible identification from a fully documented reconstruction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrales_Erforschungsnetz_au%C3%9Fergew%C3%B6hnlicher_Himmelsph%C3%A4nomene" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centrales Erforschungsnetz außergewöhnlicher Himmelsphänomene</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centrales Erforschungsnetz außergewöhnlicher Himmelsphänomene</span></span></span>

In the four publicly described Saarland cases from 2024, the proposed causes are ordinary and consistent with well-known sources of UFO reports. Yet the available news account does not publish the complete witness statements, timings, photographs or comparison data. Readers can reasonably accept the identifications as the best reported conclusions while recognising that the underlying case files are not available for independent review.

## What the Saarland record shows

CENAP’s work has weakened, rather than strengthened, claims that recent Saarland reports represent extraordinary aircraft. The identified 2024 cases were not all the same mistake: they involved astronomical misidentification, entertainment technology and an unusual aviation activity. That variety is important. There is no single object behind most regional UFO reporting; similar feelings of strangeness can arise from very different causes.

The investigations also show why precise reporting changes the outcome. A vague statement such as “a light moved strangely over Saarland” has little analytical value. Add a time, location, direction, duration and photograph with a visible horizon, and the same event may become identifiable as a star, laser projection, satellite or parachutist.

CENAP’s role in Saarland is therefore best understood as first-line civilian case triage. It receives puzzling experiences, reconstructs the sky and local activity, and separates identified events from reports that lack enough information. Its findings are not official verdicts and its archive is not a complete state catalogue. Nevertheless, in the absence of a unified public reporting system, its investigations provide one of the clearest modern records of how Saarland’s supposed UFOs are reported, tested and usually brought back into the realm of familiar phenomena.
