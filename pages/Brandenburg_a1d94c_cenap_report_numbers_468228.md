## Introduction

[Brandenburg]({{ 'why-brandenburgs-ufo-record-remains-so/' | relative_url }})’s best-known UFO statistic is straightforward but easy to misread: CENAP received **47 reports from the state in 2020**. That means 47 people or groups reported something they could not initially identify. It does not mean investigators found 47 unexplained aircraft, still less 47 vehicles of extraordinary origin. The figure measures reports entering a private investigation system, before ordinary explanations have been checked.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.moz.de/lokales/rathenow/ufo-ueber-brandenburg_-unbekannte-flugobjekte-leonard-ist-naechster-kandidat-61196141.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: moz.de">[Moz]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">moz.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ufo über Brandenburg?: Unbekannte Flugobjekte1 Dec 2021 — Im Jahr 2020 gab es aus dem Bundesland Brandenburg 47-Ufo-Meldungen, die CEN...</span></span></span>



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Brandenburg_a1d94c_cenap_report_numbers_468228-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for CENAP Reports" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

This distinction is the key to understanding Brandenburg’s UFO numbers. CENAP, the Central Research Network for Extraordinary Sky Phenomena, fills part of an information gap left by Germany’s lack of a dedicated national civilian UFO service. Its records are therefore useful evidence of what attracts attention in the sky. They are much less suitable as a simple measure of how many genuinely anomalous objects were present.

## The 2020 Brandenburg total

The figure of 47 reports was published in December 2021 by the regional newspaper *Märkische Oderzeitung*, which attributed it to CENAP co-founder Hansjürgen Köhler. The article used the number while discussing a sighting near [Rathenow]({{ 'rathenow/' | relative_url }}) in western Brandenburg, close to the dark skies of Westhavelland.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.moz.de/lokales/rathenow/ufo-ueber-brandenburg_-unbekannte-flugobjekte-leonard-ist-naechster-kandidat-61196141.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: moz.de">[Moz]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">moz.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ufo über Brandenburg?: Unbekannte Flugobjekte1 Dec 2021 — Im Jahr 2020 gab es aus dem Bundesland Brandenburg 47-Ufo-Meldungen, die CEN...</span></span></span>

Forty-seven is a relatively high level of activity for one state, but the available figure has important limits. It is an annual intake total, not a published catalogue showing the date, location, evidence and final conclusion for each Brandenburg case. Without that case-level breakdown, the number cannot reveal:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* how many reports described the same event;
* how many came from photographs rather than direct visual observations;
* how many were quickly identified;
* how many lacked enough information to investigate;
* or whether any remained unexplained after examination.

</div>

Reports are not necessarily independent events. A bright meteor, satellite train or rocket-related display visible across a wide area can generate calls from many towns. Counting each witness submission may therefore produce several reports from one physical cause. Conversely, an unusual object seen by several people who make one joint submission may appear as a single report.

The 2020 date is especially significant because Starlink satellites had recently become a major source of public confusion. Köhler later said that Starlink accounted for about 60 per cent of all reports received by CENAP that year. Shortly after launch, these satellites can appear as a striking line of lights moving together across the sky. Research into their visibility has confirmed that early Starlink spacecraft could be seen with the naked eye and that large numbers of illuminated satellites may be visible at European latitudes under favourable conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zeit.de/wissen/2025-12/ufo-meldestelle-cenap-ausserirdische-hoechststand-sichtungen/seite-3" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zeit.de">[DIE ZEIT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zeit.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Musks Unternehmen Space X schießt seine Starlink-Satelliten oft in Gruppen ins</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DIE ZEITUfo-Meldestelle CENAP: Eine neue Herausforderung: Elon...December 19, 2025 — 21 Dec 2025 — &quot;2020 waren 60 Prozent aller Meldunge...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 19, 2025</span></span></span>

That national proportion cannot simply be applied to Brandenburg’s 47 reports without the underlying case records. It does, however, show why the total should not be presented as a sudden concentration of unexplained craft. Much of CENAP’s 2020 workload was driven by a new and highly visible human-made object.

## How CENAP turns a sighting into a case

CENAP is a private, volunteer-run reporting and investigation group rather than a government agency. People contact it because they want an explanation for something unusual they have seen. In recent interviews, Köhler has said that useful reports should include the date, time, observing location, direction in the sky and duration of the event. Photographs and videos may also be added to the case material.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell-radio/ufo-meldestelle-cenap-in-den-meisten-faellen-sind-es-drohnen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO-Meldestelle CENAP: &quot;In den meisten Fällen sind es Drohnen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO-Meldestelle CENAP: &quot;In den meisten Fällen sind es Drohnen&quot;July 2, 2025 — Zum Internationalen UFO-Tag gibt der CENAP-Leiter und Grü...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

Investigators then compare the account with known sources of unusual-looking lights. For a night-time report, this can include astronomical information for the location, satellite and International Space Station passes, rocket activity and, where relevant, aircraft or helicopter movements. Images are compared with CENAP’s archive of previously identified phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.welt.de/255086178" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: welt.de">[DIE WELT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">welt.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">DIE WELTImmer mehr Deutsche glauben, Ufos zu sehen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diese Satelliten seien kurz nach dem Start am Himmel besonders auffällig, wenn sie wie auf einer Perlenkette aufgereiht losfliegen, erläu...</span></span></span>

The process separates three very different meanings of “unidentified”:

**Unidentified when reported.** This is what the intake figure measures. The witness does not know what was seen, so the event enters the system as a UFO report.

**Identified after investigation.** The observation is matched with a planet, satellite, aircraft, balloon, meteor, drone, reflection or another ordinary cause.

**Unresolved because the evidence is insufficient.** Investigators cannot make a secure identification, often because the time, direction, duration or original image is missing. Such a case has not necessarily resisted a strong investigation; it may simply lack enough data to test possible explanations.

This last category is commonly misunderstood. Köhler told SWR that CENAP had accumulated 108 “cold cases” among more than 12,000 reports over roughly half a century. He stressed that these were not evidence of extraterrestrial visits: the available information was too limited to establish the suspected explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell/baden-wuerttemberg/rekord-ufo-meldungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Sichtungen in Deutschland auf Rekord-NiveauJanuary 30, 2026 — 20 Jan 2025 — Seit Gründung sind bei der CENAP nach eigenen Angaben...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 30, 2026</span></span></span>

The distinction matters directly for Brandenburg. Even if one or more of the state’s 2020 cases were left unresolved, that would not by itself indicate an object displaying extraordinary capabilities. A meaningful claim would require the case record to show good positional information, reliable duration, consistent witness testimony and the exclusion of known astronomical, aviation and satellite causes.

## What usually creates the reports

CENAP’s later annual totals provide useful context for Brandenburg’s 47 submissions. The organisation recorded 1,084 reports from Germany, Austria and Switzerland in 2024, compared with what Köhler described as a usual annual range of 600 to 800. The total rose again to 1,348 in 2025. CENAP said the increase continued a pattern visible since 2019, while the main explanations remained familiar: planets, meteors, satellites, rocket stages, space debris and several kinds of drone. More than 120 reports that year concerned Starlink alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.welt.de/article695becd2fb77630dac278675" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: welt.de">[DIE WELT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">welt.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">DIE WELTUFO-Meldestelle verzeichnet Rekordzahl</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DIE WELTUFO-Meldestelle verzeichnet Rekordzahl</span></span></span>

These categories explain why Brandenburg can generate a substantial caseload without possessing a corresponding concentration of persistent anomalies.

Brandenburg surrounds Berlin and lies beneath busy air traffic. Aircraft approaching an observer can appear almost stationary because their motion is largely along the viewer’s line of sight. Landing lights may dominate the outline of the aircraft, particularly in haze or darkness.

The state also contains broad rural areas with open horizons and relatively dark skies. These conditions improve visibility, but they also make bright planets, satellites, meteors and distant lights more conspicuous. Westhavelland’s reputation for dark skies, for example, encourages people to look upwards and gives them a better chance of noticing unfamiliar objects. Greater observing opportunity can increase reporting even when the underlying phenomena are conventional.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.moz.de/lokales/rathenow/ufo-ueber-brandenburg_-unbekannte-flugobjekte-leonard-ist-naechster-kandidat-61196141.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: moz.de">[Moz]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">moz.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ufo über Brandenburg?: Unbekannte Flugobjekte1 Dec 2021 — Im Jahr 2020 gab es aus dem Bundesland Brandenburg 47-Ufo-Meldungen, die CEN...</span></span></span>

Modern cameras introduce another source of cases. CENAP says that some submitted photographs and videos show lens reflections or nearby insects and birds rendered as indistinct light spots. A camera can record something the witness did not consciously see, but an unexplained mark in an image is not automatically a distant object in the sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.welt.de/255086178" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: welt.de">[DIE WELT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">welt.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">DIE WELTImmer mehr Deutsche glauben, Ufos zu sehen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diese Satelliten seien kurz nach dem Start am Himmel besonders auffällig, wenn sie wie auf einer Perlenkette aufgereiht losfliegen, erläu...</span></span></span>

Drones further blur the older boundary between mundane aircraft and genuinely difficult observations. Small drones can hover, change direction abruptly and display lights that reveal little about their shape or distance. CENAP reported increasing drone identifications during the first half of the 2020s, alongside greater public attention caused by news coverage of suspected surveillance flights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.welt.de/article695becd2fb77630dac278675" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: welt.de">[DIE WELT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">welt.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">DIE WELTUFO-Meldestelle verzeichnet Rekordzahl</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DIE WELTUFO-Meldestelle verzeichnet Rekordzahl</span></span></span>

## Why rising totals do not prove rising anomalies

A report total is shaped by at least three things: what is visible, how many people notice it and how easy it is to submit an account. All three have changed.

The visible sky now contains far more conspicuous satellites than it did during the earlier decades of UFO reporting. Starlink launch groups are a clear example of technology creating a new reporting stimulus. Their orderly formation may look more artificial and therefore more puzzling than an isolated point of light, even though satellite tracking can often identify them quickly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fr.de/wissen/ufo-meldestelle-cenap-statistik-2022-ungeklaert-starlink-astronomie-himmel-ungeklaert-zr-92045530.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fr.de">[FR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on fr.de.</span></span></span>

Public awareness also affects the numbers. Reports can rise after media coverage encourages people to interpret lights as drones, surveillance devices or UFOs. CENAP said that attention to possible espionage drones made casual observers more likely to send in videos of objects that were subsequently identified as ordinary drone activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.welt.de/article695becd2fb77630dac278675" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: welt.de">[DIE WELT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">welt.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">DIE WELTUFO-Meldestelle verzeichnet Rekordzahl</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DIE WELTUFO-Meldestelle verzeichnet Rekordzahl</span></span></span>

Smartphones have lowered the practical barrier to reporting. A witness can record a short video and contact an investigator immediately rather than relying on memory or writing a letter. Köhler has linked the recent rise partly to the fact that more people carry cameras and submit images of unfamiliar lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.welt.de/255086178" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: welt.de">[DIE WELT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">welt.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">DIE WELTImmer mehr Deutsche glauben, Ufos zu sehen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diese Satelliten seien kurz nach dem Start am Himmel besonders auffällig, wenn sie wie auf einer Perlenkette aufgereiht losfliegen, erläu...</span></span></span>

These effects mean that annual totals are better understood as a measure of **reporting pressure** than of anomaly frequency. A rise may indicate clearer weather, a visible satellite launch, an unusually bright planetary arrangement, extensive press interest or easier access to the reporting service. It does not demonstrate that unidentified craft have become more numerous.

Brandenburg’s 2020 total is therefore most useful when interpreted as a snapshot of public encounters with a changing sky. It shows that residents were actively noticing and reporting unfamiliar phenomena during a year dominated nationally by Starlink cases. It cannot, without published outcomes for the individual files, establish how many observations survived ordinary explanation.

## How much confidence should readers place in the figures?

CENAP’s numbers are valuable because they document reports that might otherwise leave no lasting public trace. Germany does not operate a French-style civilian agency dedicated to collecting and publishing UFO investigations, and a Bundestag paper records the federal government’s position that it held no corresponding collection of UFO sighting files suitable for release. Private groups consequently provide much of the accessible numerical evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/406336/741fdc9b7e96b9346e4e3414225b2835/wd-8-104-09-pdf-data.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bundestag.de">[Deutscher Bundestag]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bundestag.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Deutscher Bundestag Wissenschaftliche Dienste</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Deutscher Bundestag Wissenschaftliche Dienste</span></span></span>

Private datasets nevertheless have limitations. They depend on voluntary self-reporting, so they do not capture everyone who sees something unusual. Publicity, the visibility of the reporting organisation and the witness’s willingness to contact a sceptically oriented investigator can all affect who submits a case. The totals are not a random or complete sample of Brandenburg’s population.

CENAP’s published annual figures also tend to appear through interviews and news reports rather than as a fully open, state-by-state research dataset containing every initial report and final classification. The Brandenburg figure can therefore be checked as an attributed total, but it cannot presently support detailed calculations about geographical clusters, explanation rates or long-term trends within the state.

Methodological differences between private groups create another complication. Germany’s Society for the Study of the UFO Phenomenon, or GEP, uses a shared sighting database based on a questionnaire and formal classification fields. CENAP withdrew permission for its cases to be included in that joint system in 2012. This means that the country’s civilian reports are divided among organisations rather than assembled in one standardised national dataset.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufo-forschung.de/kurz-notiert/die-gemeinsame-ufo-sichtungsdatenbank" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufo-forschung.de">[UFO Forschung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufo-forschung.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufo-forschung.de.</span></span></span>

Readers should therefore treat the 47 as a credible report-intake figure attributed to CENAP, but not as a complete census of everything unusual seen over Brandenburg in 2020. Nor should it be combined casually with figures from other organisations whose collection and classification methods may differ.

## The statistical bottom line

The safest reading of Brandenburg’s CENAP figures is also the most informative. The state generated a noticeable volume of public UFO reporting, including 47 submissions in 2020, but the number records **questions presented to investigators**, not extraordinary answers.

National CENAP data show that most of the rising caseload has been associated with visible satellites, planets, stars, aircraft, balloons, drones, meteors, rockets, space debris and camera effects. The particularly strong influence of Starlink in 2020 weakens any attempt to interpret Brandenburg’s figure as evidence of a local wave of unexplained craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zeit.de/wissen/2025-12/ufo-meldestelle-cenap-ausserirdische-hoechststand-sichtungen/seite-3" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zeit.de">[DIE ZEIT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zeit.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Musks Unternehmen Space X schießt seine Starlink-Satelliten oft in Gruppen ins</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DIE ZEITUfo-Meldestelle CENAP: Eine neue Herausforderung: Elon...December 19, 2025 — 21 Dec 2025 — &quot;2020 waren 60 Prozent aller Meldunge...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 19, 2025</span></span></span>

For Brandenburg’s UFO history, the statistic matters because it reveals how often unfamiliar appearances in the state’s skies become reportable mysteries. Its evidential limit is equally important: without transparent case outcomes, it cannot show how many reports were securely explained, too weak to assess or genuinely resistant to identification. The number measures the beginning of an investigation, not its conclusion.
