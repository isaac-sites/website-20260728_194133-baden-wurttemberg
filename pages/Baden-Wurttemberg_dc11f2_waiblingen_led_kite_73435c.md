## Introduction

Waiblingen’s celebrated “UFO alarm” was not an unexplained aircraft and was not confirmed by a police helicopter. On the evening of 21 July 2020, between ten and fifteen callers reported coloured lights over the Beinstein district. Officers at Waiblingen police station could also see the distant display, prompting ground patrols and the deployment of a helicopter. The airborne crew found nothing, but local reporting soon identified the likely source: a kite with a long, multicoloured LED-lit tail. Later photographs and a 2024 recurrence of an almost identical display made that explanation substantially stronger.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zvw.de/blaulicht/mehrere-notrufe-bei-der-polizei-ist-das-ufo-%C3%BCber-beinstein-ein-lenkdrache_arid-181913" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zvw.de">[zvw.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zvw.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache? - Polizeibericht Rems-Murr-Kreis - Zeitungsverlag Waiblingen...</span></span></span>

The case matters within Baden-Württemberg’s UFO history because it involved numerous sincere [witnesses]({{ 'witnesses-afe9a0/' | relative_url }}) and a real police response, yet still arose from an ordinary object seen under poor night-time viewing conditions. It is a compact example of why witness numbers alone do not establish that something extraordinary was in the sky.

## The calls and police response

At about 10 pm on Tuesday, 21 July, callers described an illuminated object or beams of light near Beinstein, east of central Waiblingen. Accounts referred to differently coloured lights appearing to circulate around an indistinct shape. The local newspaper reported that ten to fifteen emergency calls were made, while officers inside the Waiblingen station independently confirmed that something luminous was visible in the same direction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zvw.de/blaulicht/mehrere-notrufe-bei-der-polizei-ist-das-ufo-%C3%BCber-beinstein-ein-lenkdrache_arid-181913" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zvw.de">[ZVW]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zvw.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache? - Polizeibericht Rems-Murr-Kreis - Zeitungsverlag Waiblingen...</span></span></span>

The officers’ observation is important, but only in a limited sense. It shows that the reports were not invented, a social-media hoax or a hallucination shared only through suggestion. A physical light source was present. It does not show that the initial descriptions of its size, height or nature were accurate.

Distance made the object’s outline difficult to judge. A police spokesman relayed estimates of roughly ten to fifteen metres in size and about 100 metres above the ground. Those figures were not measurements from radar, photography or a known reference point; they were visual impressions of lights against a dark sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zvw.de/blaulicht/mehrere-notrufe-bei-der-polizei-ist-das-ufo-%C3%BCber-beinstein-ein-lenkdrache_arid-181913" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zvw.de">[ZVW]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zvw.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache? - Polizeibericht Rems-Murr-Kreis - Zeitungsverlag Waiblingen...</span></span></span>

Patrols were sent towards Beinstein but could not locate the source. At around 10.30 pm, the lights were last reported moving northwards or disappearing from view. A police helicopter then flew over the area without making any further observation. Contemporary police-derived reporting therefore does **not** support the later shorthand that the helicopter itself saw or tracked the UFO. It was deployed because of the reports and the possible aviation-safety implications, but it failed to find the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkz.de/nachrichten/unbekanntes-flugobjekt-gesichtet-78437.html?bPrint=true" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkz.de">[Backnanger Kreiszeitung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkz.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Backnanger Kreiszeitung Artikel Druckansicht</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Backnanger Kreiszeitung Artikel Druckansicht</span></span></span>

Police treated the event cautiously rather than as evidence of alien activity. Early possibilities included a drone, several drones or a kite. That was a reasonable operational response: an unidentified illuminated object could potentially interfere with aircraft or indicate unauthorised drone activity even when its eventual explanation is mundane.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zvw.de/blaulicht/mehrere-notrufe-bei-der-polizei-ist-das-ufo-%C3%BCber-beinstein-ein-lenkdrache_arid-181913" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zvw.de">[ZVW]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zvw.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache? - Polizeibericht Rems-Murr-Kreis - Zeitungsverlag Waiblingen...</span></span></span>

## The illuminated kite explanation

The decisive lead came from Beinstein resident Maik Kasper, who said he had observed the object earlier in the evening from his balcony, when there was enough light to recognise it as a kite. He described a nylon tail approximately five or six metres long, fitted with LEDs that changed between green, pink, yellow and blue. He believed the line had probably been fixed somewhere between Beinstein’s church and Waiblingen’s festival ground because the kite remained aloft for hours.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zvw.de/blaulicht/mehrere-notrufe-bei-der-polizei-ist-das-ufo-%C3%BCber-beinstein-ein-lenkdrache_arid-181913" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zvw.de">[ZVW]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zvw.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache? - Polizeibericht Rems-Murr-Kreis - Zeitungsverlag Waiblingen...</span></span></span>

This account matched several otherwise puzzling features:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **The colours:** Changing LEDs naturally explain the reported ring, chain or sequence of multicoloured lights.
* **The slow, floating movement:** A tethered kite can hover, sway and drift without following the direct path expected of an aircraft.
* **The unclear outline:** At night, observers could see the lights while the kite, line and tail remained largely invisible.
* **The apparent size:** Separated lights on a moving tail can be interpreted as the boundary of one much larger object.
* **The long duration:** A secured line would allow the display to remain in approximately the same area without someone continuously holding it.

</div>

Initial coverage correctly treated Kasper’s identification as a witness explanation rather than a formally established police finding. ## Why the witnesses misjudged it

The Waiblingen witnesses need not have been careless or credulous. Night-time lights are unusually difficult to interpret because observers lose many of the visual cues normally used to calculate distance and scale. A bright point may belong to a small nearby object or a much larger distant one. Without seeing the supporting kite, its tether or the landscape beneath it, a witness has no reliable basis for converting angular size into metres.

This explains how officers could genuinely see the phenomenon while patrols struggled to find it. Observers at the station knew its direction but not its precise distance. A kite that appeared to be above a particular street or field could have been nearer or farther away than assumed. Changes in wind, viewing angle and intervening buildings would further complicate attempts to reach the apparent position.

The reported estimate of a ten-to-fifteen-metre object illustrates the problem. Kasper’s description instead concerned a much smaller kite with a five-to-six-metre illuminated tail. The difference does not indicate dishonesty. It shows how easily the brain groups several lights into the imagined outline of a solid craft, particularly when the dark structure connecting them cannot be seen.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zvw.de/blaulicht/mehrere-notrufe-bei-der-polizei-ist-das-ufo-%C3%BCber-beinstein-ein-lenkdrache_arid-181913" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zvw.de">[ZVW]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zvw.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache? - Polizeibericht Rems-Murr-Kreis - Zeitungsverlag Waiblingen...</span></span></span>

Multiple witnesses also do not provide fully independent measurements when they view the same ambiguous stimulus under similar conditions. Ten people can accurately agree that coloured lights are present while sharing the same mistaken assumptions about their distance, dimensions and movement. The Waiblingen reports corroborated the existence of the lights; they did not independently establish the existence of a ten-metre vehicle.

## What the case teaches about UFO evidence

Waiblingen is best classified as an explained identification case rather than an enduring mystery. Its value lies less in the object itself than in the sequence by which an ordinary display temporarily became an official UFO alarm.

First, “UFO” initially meant exactly what the term says: the object had not yet been identified. Police deployment was evidence that the report was taken seriously as a possible safety matter, not official endorsement of an extraordinary craft.

Second, the strongest evidence emerged from a witness who saw the phenomenon earlier and under better lighting. His description accounted for the colours, motion and duration with one familiar object. Later photographic reporting strengthened the identification, while the similar 2024 appearance showed that illuminated kites were not merely a hypothetical explanation invented after the event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zvw.de/blaulicht/mehrere-notrufe-bei-der-polizei-ist-das-ufo-%C3%BCber-beinstein-ein-lenkdrache_arid-181913" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zvw.de">[ZVW]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zvw.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mehrere Notrufe bei der Polizei: Ist das Ufo über Beinstein ein Lenkdrache? - Polizeibericht Rems-Murr-Kreis - Zeitungsverlag Waiblingen...</span></span></span>

Third, the helicopter’s failure to locate the display should not be converted into a mysterious “vanishing” or an assumption that the object evaded detection. By the time it searched, the lights had already last been seen moving away or disappearing. The simplest interpretation is that the kite descended, shifted position, was obscured or was removed before the crew arrived. No radar trace, exceptional manoeuvre, physical remnant or aviation encounter was reported.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkz.de/nachrichten/unbekanntes-flugobjekt-gesichtet-78437.html?bPrint=true" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkz.de">[Backnanger Kreiszeitung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkz.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Backnanger Kreiszeitung Artikel Druckansicht</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Backnanger Kreiszeitung Artikel Druckansicht</span></span></span>

Within Baden-Württemberg’s broader UFO record, the incident is therefore a useful warning against ranking cases solely by the number or status of witnesses. Residents and police officers can all report accurately that they saw something unusual while being mistaken about what produced it. Waiblingen’s “UFO” was real as an observed light phenomenon and real as a police alarm, but the later evidence points firmly to a tethered kite decorated with coloured LEDs—not an unresolved aircraft.
