## Introduction

A strange shape in a photograph can look more impressive than the event that produced it. In [Hesse]({{ 'why-hesses-ufo-story-is-mostly-about/' | relative_url }}), where the Lützelbach-based UFO reporting centre [CENAP]({{ 'cenap/' | relative_url }}) receives images from across Germany, many apparently unusual photographs have proved to be lens reflections, nearby insects, birds, balloons or ordinary lights recorded out of focus. The image preserves a bright patch or dark silhouette, but often loses the distance, scale, direction and motion needed to identify it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Weitere Meldungen im Jahr 2024 konnten als LED-Ballons, Folienballons, Privat- und Industrie-Drohnen, Flugzeuge, Hubschrauber, Lichteffek...</span></span></span>

That is the central problem: a photograph records how light reached a camera sensor, not a complete three-dimensional account of what was in the sky. A tiny insect close to the lens and a much larger object far away may occupy a similar number of pixels. A reflection inside the camera may resemble a separate luminous object. Blur can erase the features that would distinguish a bird from a disc. Investigators therefore treat a photograph as one piece of evidence, not as automatic proof that a solid, distant craft was present.

## Lens reflections create objects that were never in the sky

Bright light entering a camera can reflect between optical surfaces inside the lens. Camera manufacturers call the resulting effects flare and ghosting. Canon describes ghosting as an internal lens reflection, while Nikon explains that strong light entering at certain angles can generate reflections that coatings are designed to suppress. These artefacts may appear as coloured discs, streaks, polygons or pale duplicates of a bright source elsewhere in the frame.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canon-europe.com/cameras/eos-5d-mark-iv/dual-pixel-raw/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canon-europe.com">[Canon Europe]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canon-europe.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Canon Europe Dual Pixel RAW</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Canon EuropeDual Pixel RAW - Canon EOS 5D Mark IVUnlike lens flare, which can sometimes be used creatively in making an image appear low...</span></span></span>

This is especially relevant to photographs taken at night in urban or suburban Hesse. Street lamps, illuminated buildings, vehicle lights, the Moon and event lighting can sit just inside or outside the frame. The witness may concentrate on a bright object in the sky and notice a second “object” only after inspecting the photograph. CENAP reported that many submitted images examined during the record reporting year of 2024 were largely attributable to lens reflections or to nearby birds and insects rendered out of focus.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Weitere Meldungen im Jahr 2024 konnten als LED-Ballons, Folienballons, Privat- und Industrie-Drohnen, Flugzeuge, Hubschrauber, Lichteffek...</span></span></span>

A lens reflection has no independent distance, size or flight path because it is generated within the optical system. Several clues can nevertheless make the explanation testable:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The suspected object may lie on a line passing through the centre of the image and a bright lamp or the Sun.
* Its position may shift when the camera angle changes, even though the real light source stays fixed.
* Its colour and shape may resemble other flare elements in the frame.
* It may appear in the photograph although the witness did not see it directly.

</div>

None of these signs proves that every luminous patch is flare. They show why investigators need the uncropped original image, the position of bright sources and, ideally, several frames taken while the camera was moved. NASA’s independent UAP study specifically recommended modelling optical ghosting caused by solar or lunar glints within camera systems, because such sensor effects can be mistaken for external objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report-0.pdf?emrc=69d6ebd6a1bf9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasa.gov">[NASA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">uap independent study team final report 0</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">uap independent study team final report 0</span></span></span>

## Blurred birds and insects become unfamiliar shapes

Nearby flying animals are among the most misleading photographic subjects because they can cross the camera’s field of view in a fraction of a second. An insect may be only centimetres from the lens, far outside the distance on which the camera is focused. Its body and wings then merge into an oval, rod, crescent or translucent smear. A bird farther away may become a dark disc or boomerang when wing movement and camera shake are combined.

The effect is strongest when the sky is bright and featureless. The camera focuses near infinity, while a moth, fly or bird passes through the foreground. Because the animal is blurred, familiar details such as legs, feathers and wing edges disappear. Motion blur can also stretch the image along the direction of travel. Digital image specialists note that moving subjects may be distorted further by rolling-shutter sensors, which record successive rows of an image at slightly different times rather than capturing the whole frame instantaneously.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://enfsi.eu/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/BPM_Image-Authentication_ENFSI-BPM-DI-003-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: enfsi.eu">[ENFSI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">enfsi.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BPM Image Authentication ENFSI BPM DI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BPM Image Authentication ENFSI BPM DI</span></span></span>

A nearby insect can also appear surprisingly large. The picture does not announce that the object was twenty centimetres from the phone rather than hundreds of metres away. Without depth cues, viewers may unconsciously place the shape in the distant sky and then infer a much larger physical object. This is one reason CENAP’s finding about out-of-focus insects and birds matters more than a simple list of debunked photographs: it identifies a repeatable mechanism by which an ordinary foreground object acquires the apparent scale of an aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Weitere Meldungen im Jahr 2024 konnten als LED-Ballons, Folienballons, Privat- und Industrie-Drohnen, Flugzeuge, Hubschrauber, Lichteffek...</span></span></span>

Investigators look for features such as extreme softness compared with the background, partial transparency, repeated wing-like lobes, a trail produced by motion blur, or a shape that changes radically between adjacent frames. A burst of photographs is much more useful than a single still. In one frame the animal may look like a smooth disc; in the next, its wings or irregular path may become visible.

## The photograph usually has no built-in scale

The most serious weakness in many UFO photographs is not poor sharpness but missing geometry. A camera records angular size: how much of the frame an object occupies. It does not directly reveal the object’s true width or its distance from the camera. Apparent size depends on both. A small nearby object can therefore produce the same image size as a large distant one. Basic astronomical measurement uses the same principle: an object’s apparent angular size is related jointly to its actual size and its distance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lweb.cfa.harvard.edu/webscope/activities/pdfs/measureSize.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lweb.cfa.harvard.edu">[Center for Astrophysics]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lweb.cfa.harvard.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for AstrophysicsMeasuring Size from Images: A wrangle with angles and...The key to using telescope images to measure distances is...</span></span></span>

To calculate real size reliably, an investigator needs additional information. This might include a known reference object at the same distance, an accurately measured range, several images from separated positions, or simultaneous observations from different locations. Research on smartphone measurement likewise shows that determining both size and distance requires more than one viewpoint or another known quantity; a lone image generally leaves the two variables entangled.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.05319" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Determination of the transverse width and distance of an object with a smartphone cameraAugust 9, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 9, 2022</span></span></span>

This explains why dramatic estimates made from a photograph alone are often circular. A commentator assumes that a blur was above a distant ridge, calculates that it must have been tens of metres wide, and then treats the large calculated size as proof that it could not have been a bird. But the calculation depended on the unproved assumption that the blur was near the ridge rather than close to the camera.

Forced perspective and parallax create similar errors in moving footage. The US All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office notes that an observer or sensor in motion may see a stationary or slow object apparently sweep across a distant background. When its range is uncertain, estimates of size and speed can be badly distorted.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/Information%20Papers/AARO_Effect_of_Forced_Perspective_and_Parallax_View_on_UAP_Observations_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Effect of Forced Perspective and Parallax View on UAP Observations</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Effect of Forced Perspective and Parallax View on UAP Observations...</span></span></span> A widely discussed example is the “GoFast” military video: NASA found that display data supported a much less extraordinary interpretation than the initial impression of an object racing just above the sea, while later AARO analysis attributed the apparent high speed to motion parallax.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report-0.pdf?emrc=69d6ebd6a1bf9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasa.gov">[NASA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">uap independent study team final report 0</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">uap independent study team final report 0</span></span></span>

The example is not a Hessian case, but the lesson applies directly to images submitted from Hesse. A photograph of a featureless light above [Frankfurt]({{ 'frankfurt/' | relative_url }}), the Odenwald or a rural horizon cannot establish exceptional speed or size unless the camera position, viewing direction, timing and object range are independently constrained.

## A sharp-looking image may still contain very little information

Modern phones can make an ambiguous photograph look cleaner without making it more informative. Night modes may combine several exposures, align them and apply noise reduction or sharpening. Digital zoom enlarges pixels rather than recovering detail that the lens never resolved. Messaging platforms and social media may resize or recompress the file. The result may have clearer edges and stronger contrast, but those edges can reflect processing as much as the original scene.

Forensic guidance therefore distinguishes the original image from later copies. The European Network of Forensic Science Institutes defines an original as an image whose integrity has been preserved since creation, and notes that lossy formats such as JPEG reduce image quality. Its guidance treats file structure, metadata, compression traces, perspective and geometry as parts of a proper authentication examination.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://enfsi.eu/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/BPM_Image-Authentication_ENFSI-BPM-DI-003-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: enfsi.eu">[ENFSI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">enfsi.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BPM Image Authentication ENFSI BPM DI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BPM Image Authentication ENFSI BPM DI</span></span></span>

Metadata can provide the capture time, camera model, focal length, exposure and sometimes location. It does not identify the object by itself, and metadata can be absent or altered, but losing it removes valuable constraints. NASA found that UAP images frequently lack information about sensor type, acquisition time, exposure, location and other operating conditions. Even where photographs or videos exist, those gaps often prevent a conclusive determination of size, motion or nature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report-0.pdf?emrc=69d6ebd6a1bf9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasa.gov">[NASA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">uap independent study team final report 0</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">uap independent study team final report 0</span></span></span>

For a Hessian report, the difference between an original phone file and a cropped screenshot may therefore determine whether an [investigation]({{ 'investigation-629971/' | relative_url }}) can progress. The screenshot may preserve the mysterious shape while deleting the very information needed to test aircraft routes, astronomical positions, satellite passes or camera artefacts.

## What makes a photograph genuinely useful

A useful UFO photograph is not necessarily the sharpest or most dramatic. It is the one embedded in a well-documented observation. Investigators gain much more from a modest image with an exact time and location than from a spectacular blur whose origin and processing history are unknown.

The strongest submissions include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the untouched original file rather than a social-media copy;
* several frames before and after the object appeared;
* the precise time, location and viewing direction;
* a wide image showing the horizon and surrounding light sources;
* a description of what was visible to the eye, separate from what appeared only in the picture;
* camera movement, zoom and focus information;
* another witness or image from a different position.

</div>

Multiple near-simultaneous observations are particularly valuable because they may allow triangulation of location, velocity and size. NASA’s UAP study proposed standardised smartphone reporting for this reason: several viewpoints with retained metadata can convert an ambiguous image into measurable evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report-0.pdf?emrc=69d6ebd6a1bf9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasa.gov">[NASA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">uap independent study team final report 0</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">uap independent study team final report 0</span></span></span>

This does not mean that every poorly documented Hessian photograph is worthless. It may still show that the witness photographed a real light or passing object. The limit is narrower but important: the image may be unable to establish what the object was, how far away it was or whether its movement was unusual.

## The correct verdict is often “insufficient”, not “extraordinary”

Photographs attract attention because they appear more objective than memory. Yet a camera can introduce its own ambiguities while stripping away depth, movement and context. Lens reflections can produce a luminous “object” that did not exist outside the camera. Birds and insects can become smooth discs or elongated forms. Missing scale can turn a nearby speck into an apparently enormous craft. Compression and enhancement can make the result look definite without adding reliable detail.

For Hesse’s UFO history, this is a recurring investigative lesson rather than a minor technical footnote. The high volume of images reaching CENAP’s Lützelbach reporting centre has supplied repeated examples of ordinary subjects transformed by optics and recording conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Weitere Meldungen im Jahr 2024 konnten als LED-Ballons, Folienballons, Privat- und Industrie-Drohnen, Flugzeuge, Hubschrauber, Lichteffek...</span></span></span> The sensible conclusion is not that all unusual photographs are explained in advance. It is that a strange appearance, by itself, carries very little evidential weight.

A photograph becomes persuasive only when it helps investigators test competing explanations. Where the original file, geometry and contextual data are missing, the most accurate classification may remain “[unresolved]({{ 'unresolved/' | relative_url }}) through lack of information”. That is a statement about the limits of the evidence, not evidence that an extraordinary aircraft was present.
