That does not mean every Hessian report has been conclusively reconstructed. Some remain open because witnesses reported them late, supplied only a distant light or blurred image, or could not provide enough positional and timing data. In practice, “[unresolved]({{ 'unresolved/' | relative_url }})” usually means that the evidence is incomplete, not that investigators have established an object with impossible capabilities. Hesse’s real contribution to German UFO history is therefore methodological: it shows how unusual experiences become reports, how reporting waves follow changes in technology and public attention, and how difficult it is to turn sincere testimony into reliable proof.

## Hesse’s unusual place in German UFO history

The strongest state-level connection is the UFO reporting centre operated by the Central Research Network for Extraordinary Aerial Phenomena, commonly known by its German initials, [CENAP]({{ 'cenap/' | relative_url }}). The organisation began in the Mannheim area in the 1970s, but co-founder Hansjürgen Köhler has run its reporting work from Lützelbach in southern Hesse since the 1990s. Its archive and telephone service have made this small Odenwald community a national destination for people seeking explanations for strange lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity...</span></span></span>

CENAP is not a government [investigation]({{ 'investigation-629971/' | relative_url }}) unit, police department or university institute. It is a private, volunteer-led organisation with a strongly sceptical approach. Reports are checked against astronomical conditions, satellite passes, aircraft and helicopter movements, rocket activity and a large collection of earlier cases. Investigators record basic details such as date, time, location and viewing direction before comparing photographs or video with known visual patterns.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hessenschau.de.</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because figures reported by the centre are often misunderstood. CENAP’s annual totals cover reports from across Germany and frequently neighbouring countries; they are not counts of spacecraft seen over Hesse. The organisation received 1,084 reports in 2024 and 1,348 in 2025, its highest total to that point, but said that none of the 2025 cases remained genuinely unidentified after examination. More than 120 of those reports concerned Starlink satellites alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hessenschau.de.</span></span></span>

Hesse is consequently both a source of sightings and a centre where sightings from elsewhere are filtered and reclassified. That gives the state a prominent role in German UFO culture even though its own case history contains comparatively little firm evidence for a major unexplained incident.

## The recurring pattern around Frankfurt and southern Hesse

Local press mapping has shown a concentration of reports in southern Hesse, particularly around [Frankfurt]({{ 'frankfurt/' | relative_url }}). Witness descriptions have included expanding red lights, formations, [fireballs]({{ 'fireballs/' | relative_url }}) and luminous boomerang-like shapes. Yet the same reporting noted that investigators were able to identify the great majority of submitted cases as familiar phenomena such as balloons, reflections, aircraft trails or astronomical objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fnp.de/frankfurt/ueber-diesen-orten-hessen-wurden-ufos-beobachtet-10425599.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fnp.de">[FNP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fnp.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Über diesen Orten in Hessen wurden Ufos beobachtet</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FNPÜber diesen Orten in Hessen wurden Ufos beobachtet...</span></span></span>

Frankfurt naturally produces more ambiguous aerial observations than many rural areas. It combines a large population, heavy air traffic, illuminated buildings, event lighting and views across a broad urban horizon. A light that appears stationary may be an approaching aircraft seen almost head-on; one that suddenly vanishes may have turned, entered cloud or switched off its landing lights. Bright advertising or entertainment beams can appear as moving patches where they strike low cloud.

A clear example occurred when unexplained shafts of light were reported across the Rhine-Main area in February 2024. They were subsequently attributed to powerful entertainment searchlights rather than objects moving within the clouds. Similar photographs submitted to CENAP have turned out to contain lens reflections, while small out-of-focus birds or insects near a camera have appeared as large indistinct forms against the sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hessenschau.de.</span></span></span>

These explanations are sometimes dismissed as too ordinary, but they fit an important feature of the reports: many describe lights without dependable information about distance, size or speed. A witness cannot calculate an object’s true velocity merely from how quickly a point of light crosses their field of view. A nearby insect, a distant aircraft and a satellite can produce radically different physical events while leaving superficially similar images.

## The Gießen cases show how investigations change a story

Reports from the Gießen district provide one of the clearest small-scale histories of UFO investigation in Hesse. According to Köhler, the earliest cases in the local record came from 1982 and 1986. Witnesses saw orange-red fireballs hovering or moving for several minutes. The reports sounded unusual, particularly before modern consumer drones became common, but investigators concluded that the lights were small hot-air lanterns whose flames and drifting movement created the impression of glowing aerial objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.giessener-allgemeine.de/kreis-giessen/ufo-giessen-kreis-sichtung-licht-flugzeuge-90857168.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: giessener-allgemeine.de">[Gießener Allgemeine Zeitung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">giessener-allgemeine.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on giessener-allgemeine.de.</span></span></span>

A more dramatic incident occurred near [Fernwald]({{ 'fernwald/' | relative_url }}) in 2001. Young adults travelling on the B457 saw a bright light apparently approaching their car and becoming increasingly intense. They stopped but were afraid to get out. After switching off the engine, they heard a faint humming sound; the light then crossed fields, dimmed and disappeared. The eventual identification was a German military helicopter. Civil air-traffic information helped the inquiry, although investigators said details of military movements were more difficult to obtain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.giessener-allgemeine.de/kreis-giessen/ufo-giessen-kreis-sichtung-licht-flugzeuge-90857168.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: giessener-allgemeine.de">[Gießener Allgemeine Zeitung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">giessener-allgemeine.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on giessener-allgemeine.de.</span></span></span>

This case illustrates why witness sincerity and witness accuracy are separate questions. The people in the car may have described their experience honestly. Darkness, an uncertain horizon, fear and a vehicle’s enclosed acoustic environment nevertheless made an ordinary aircraft seem much stranger. The identification does not require the witnesses to have invented anything.

Another regional event was genuinely extraterrestrial in the literal sense, though not in the popular UFO sense. On 16 June 2018, people in Biebertal, Lahnau, Großen-Buseck and near Schiffenberg saw a fast blue-green fireball moving from west to east. Investigators identified it as a bright meteor. The incident demonstrates how a spectacular object from space can be real while providing no evidence of an intelligently controlled craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.giessener-allgemeine.de/kreis-giessen/ufo-giessen-kreis-sichtung-licht-flugzeuge-90857168.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: giessener-allgemeine.de">[Gießener Allgemeine Zeitung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">giessener-allgemeine.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on giessener-allgemeine.de.</span></span></span>

Together, these cases form a useful progression:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **The 1980s fireballs** were unusual-looking lanterns.
* **The Fernwald approach light** was a military helicopter.
* **The 2018 blue-green object** was a natural object entering Earth’s atmosphere.

</div>

None was a hoax, and none required an exotic explanation.

## Frankfurt Airport turns identification into a safety issue

Around an airport, an unidentified object is more than a curiosity. Even a small drone can force controllers to change approaches, delay aircraft or temporarily restrict a section of airspace. In such cases, “unidentified” is an operational category: authorities must respond before they know whether the object is a drone, balloon, aircraft or mistaken observation.

In July 2024, passenger pilots reported an orange drone near Offenbach while approaching Frankfurt Airport. A second sighting followed in the Neu-Isenburg area. Police operations briefly affected air traffic, and investigators opened inquiries into possible interference with aviation. The reports did not involve unexplained high-performance craft; they concerned suspected drones whose operators had not initially been located.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/43561/5814661" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: presseportal.de">[Presseportal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">presseportal.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">war der Flugverkehr am Flughafen Frankfurt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ermittlungen nach Drohnen-Sichtungen: Polizei weist auf...July 2, 2024 — 2 Jul 2024 — Ermittlungen nach Drohnen-Sichtungen...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2024</span></span></span>

In October 2025, police identified and detained the operator of another drone flown within Frankfurt Airport’s restricted zone. The aircraft was seized and normal airport operations continued. The incident shows how an alarming “unknown object” can become an ordinary enforcement matter once the operator and equipment are found.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.welt.de/article68e115cac9fcb483178b77c8" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: welt.de">[DIE WELT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">welt.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">DIE WELTDrohne am Flughafen Frankfurt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Die Polizei konnte den Drohnenpiloten, einen 41-jährigen Mann, rasch festnehmen und die Drohne sicherstellen. The airport setting can also produce reports from experienced observers. Pilots and controllers are trained to recognise traffic, but they still encounter unfamiliar satellite reflections, unusual lighting angles and objects outside normal flight patterns. CENAP has described a case in which air-traffic personnel sought help after pilots reported dancing lights near the horizon; the lights were identified as sunlight reflecting from Starlink satellites.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity...</span></span></span>

Pilot testimony deserves attention because aviation professionals understand aircraft behaviour better than most witnesses. It is not automatically decisive, however. At night, even trained observers may lack a visible horizon, a known distance or a radar return. A detailed report supported by several aircraft, radar data and recorded communications would carry much more weight than an isolated visual observation.

## Military geography adds ambiguity, not automatic proof

Cold War Hesse contained extensive NATO and United States military infrastructure. Rhein-Main Air Base occupied part of Frankfurt Airport, while eastern Hesse faced the inner German border and the Fulda Gap, one of NATO’s most closely watched possible invasion routes. Observation posts, helicopters and military exercises were therefore part of the state’s aerial environment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhein-Main_Air_Base" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rhein-Main Air Base</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rhein-Main Air Base</span></span></span>

This setting can make military explanations both plausible and frustrating. A witness may accurately report an aircraft behaving in an unfamiliar way, yet civilian investigators may be unable to obtain its mission, route or equipment details. That was the difficulty in the Fernwald helicopter case: the broad identification was possible, but the reason for the flight was not publicly established.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.giessener-allgemeine.de/kreis-giessen/ufo-giessen-kreis-sichtung-licht-flugzeuge-90857168.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: giessener-allgemeine.de">[Gießener Allgemeine Zeitung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">giessener-allgemeine.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on giessener-allgemeine.de.</span></span></span>

It would be a mistake, however, to treat missing military information as evidence of non-human technology. Restricted information creates an evidence gap, not a conclusion. During the Cold War, Hesse’s skies contained transport aircraft, tactical helicopters, training flights, border-surveillance activity and radar installations. Any historical claim involving a military zone must first be tested against that dense background.

The public sources examined for Hesse do not reveal a landmark state incident supported simultaneously by authenticated radar records, physical traces, multiple independent witness groups and a documented official inquiry. Military associations certainly make some reports more interesting, but the available evidence does not turn them into confirmed encounters.

## Why sighting waves keep changing

Hesse’s reporting record follows broader changes in what is visible overhead. Different periods have produced different dominant “UFOs”.

**Lanterns and balloons** generated slow-moving orange lights, especially before small drones became widespread. Their apparent formation, silent movement and disappearance when the flame died could look highly structured.

**Aircraft and helicopters** remain common sources of approach lights, hovering impressions and unexplained noise. During the sharp reduction in commercial flying in 2020, CENAP reported that some observers had become less accustomed to recognising aircraft and illuminated condensation trails.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.giessener-allgemeine.de/kreis-giessen/ufo-giessen-kreis-sichtung-licht-flugzeuge-90857168.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: giessener-allgemeine.de">[Gießener Allgemeine Zeitung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">giessener-allgemeine.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on giessener-allgemeine.de.</span></span></span>

**Starlink satellites** transformed the pattern after 2019. Newly launched satellites travel in conspicuous lines that resemble a procession of evenly spaced lights. CENAP attributed 602 of Germany’s 895 reports in 2020 to Starlink and continued to identify it as a leading cause during the record reporting years that followed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.giessener-allgemeine.de/kreis-giessen/ufo-giessen-kreis-sichtung-licht-flugzeuge-90857168.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: giessener-allgemeine.de">[Gießener Allgemeine Zeitung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">giessener-allgemeine.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on giessener-allgemeine.de.</span></span></span>

**Planets and stars** generate repeated reports because atmospheric turbulence makes bright points flicker, change colour or appear to move. In one case, police officers who had been sent to examine a persistent flashing object were also initially puzzled; CENAP identified it as Sirius. Venus, Jupiter and unusual planetary pairings have likewise triggered clusters of calls.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity...</span></span></span>

**Drones** now create a more serious category. Unlike a planet or reflection, a drone may be a real aircraft violating restricted airspace. Its presence does not support an extraterrestrial interpretation, but it may require police action and can remain unidentified if the operator escapes. The rise of cheap consumer drones has therefore blurred the line between a classic UFO report and a modern aviation-security incident.

Increasing totals should not be read simply as increasing aerial activity. Smartphones allow more people to photograph and submit observations, satellite constellations have introduced unfamiliar displays, and intense media coverage encourages witnesses to look up and report. CENAP stated that its reports rose each year from 2019 through 2025, while its identification work continued to find ordinary causes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hessenschau.de.</span></span></span>

## How strong is the Hessian evidence?

The most useful way to assess Hesse’s cases is to separate them into three evidential categories.

### Explained reports

These have a plausible source matched to the time, direction and reported behaviour. The Gießen lanterns, Fernwald military helicopter, 2018 meteor, Rhine-Main searchlights and numerous Starlink observations belong here. The original experience may have been impressive, but later information substantially weakens an extraordinary interpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.giessener-allgemeine.de/kreis-giessen/ufo-giessen-kreis-sichtung-licht-flugzeuge-90857168.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: giessener-allgemeine.de">[Gießener Allgemeine Zeitung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">giessener-allgemeine.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on giessener-allgemeine.de.</span></span></span>

### Weak or insufficient reports

These consist mainly of distant lights, retrospective testimony or photographs without dependable scale and location data. They may remain technically unresolved because investigators cannot reconstruct the sky after the fact. CENAP itself says that most of its unsolved cases lack enough information for a final identification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity...</span></span></span>

Such reports should not be called debunked when no explanation has been demonstrated. Equally, they should not be presented as strong anomalies. An unexplained blurred light is evidence that identification failed, not evidence that the object defied known physics.

### Potentially significant cases

A genuinely strong Hessian case would need more than an unusual narrative. The most valuable evidence would include original time-stamped imagery, exact viewing positions, independent witnesses separated geographically, unedited radar or air-traffic records, and measurements allowing distance and speed to be calculated. Scientific proposals for studying unusual aerial phenomena emphasise precisely this combination of multiple cameras, triangulation, radar, radio, acoustic and environmental sensors because one visual channel is highly vulnerable to error.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18566" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on arxiv.org.</span></span></span>

No widely documented Hesse case in the accessible record presently meets that standard. That conclusion does not prove that every witness saw an identified object; it means that the surviving reports are not strong enough to establish an extraordinary one.

## What later reporting has changed

Later reporting has weakened rather than strengthened the most dramatic interpretation of Hesse’s UFO history. Older local cases have acquired conventional explanations, while recent reporting waves have tracked recognisable technological changes. Lanterns dominated some earlier orange-light reports; Starlink produced mass sightings after 2019; drones now account for an increasing share of aviation alerts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.giessener-allgemeine.de/kreis-giessen/ufo-giessen-kreis-sichtung-licht-flugzeuge-90857168.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: giessener-allgemeine.de">[Gießener Allgemeine Zeitung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">giessener-allgemeine.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on giessener-allgemeine.de.</span></span></span>

The record also shows the value of rapid reporting. Investigators can check aircraft routes, satellite passes, weather, astronomical positions and public events far more effectively on the same night than several years later. Delayed memories may preserve the emotional force of an encounter while losing the timing and direction needed to test it.

Hesse therefore matters less as the setting of a single unsolved mystery than as a sustained case study in how UFO reports are created and resolved. Its airports, military history, urban lighting, open rural skies and locally based reporting archive have produced a varied collection of sincere experiences. The best-supported conclusion is not that nothing unusual has ever been seen, but that “unusual” and “unexplained” are temporary descriptions. In the great majority of documented Hessian cases, better information has moved the story towards aircraft, astronomy, atmospheric effects or human technology rather than towards an unknown craft.

      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
