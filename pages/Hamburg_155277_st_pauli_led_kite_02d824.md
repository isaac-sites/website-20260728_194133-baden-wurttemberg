## Introduction

The St Pauli lights were not an unexplained aircraft. On the evening of 5 March 2013, dozens of callers reported coloured lights moving above [Hamburg]({{ 'why-hamburgs-ufo-mysteries-usually-fade/' | relative_url }})’s Heiligengeistfeld. Police officers sent to the scene could see the display but could not immediately identify its source. After searching for nearly two hours, they found a local kite enthusiast flying a three-metre-wide kite at roughly 60 metres, fitted with 120 red, green and blue LED lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bild.de/regional/hamburg/ufo/ueber-hamburg-st-pauli-29394640.bild.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bild.de">[BILD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bild.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de...</span></span></span>



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Hamburg_155277_st_pauli_led_kite_02d824-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for St Pauli Kite" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

The incident matters within Hamburg’s UFO history because it was a genuine mass sighting with police involvement, yet it was also conclusively explained while the object was still in the air. It demonstrates how darkness can conceal an ordinary object’s body, line and operator, leaving only a moving pattern of lights that appears larger, more distant and more technologically unusual than it really is.

## The police calls over Heiligengeistfeld

At about 8.02 pm, Hamburg police began receiving calls from people who believed that something resembling a UFO was flying above the Heiligengeistfeld in St Pauli. The field is a large, mostly open event space surrounded by busy roads, housing and prominent city landmarks. Its size and open sky make it a natural place for kite flying, but they also allow elevated lights to be seen from numerous surrounding streets and buildings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bild.de/regional/hamburg/ufo/ueber-hamburg-st-pauli-29394640.bild.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bild.de">[BILD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bild.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de...</span></span></span>

Three police vehicles were reportedly dispatched. This was not simply a case of one distant witness misreading a light through a window: attending officers also saw the coloured lights. Their initial inability to explain them gave the event a brief appearance of official mystery. Yet the officers did not report radar evidence, an aircraft emergency, unusual sound, physical traces or performance beyond that of an object moving in the wind. They were dealing with a visible but unidentified light display rather than evidence of an unknown machine.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bild.de/regional/hamburg/ufo/ueber-hamburg-st-pauli-29394640.bild.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bild.de">[BILD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bild.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de...</span></span></span>

The eventual source was a 63-year-old local man identified in contemporary reporting as Manfred S. He had launched a kite approximately three metres across and attached 120 LEDs in three colours. He said he had added the lights so that he could see the kite more easily at night and had not expected it to trigger a UFO alarm. The operator was described as an engineer and an experienced enthusiast who owned around 300 kites, making a deliberately constructed and controllable display considerably more plausible than an accidental tangle of lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bild.de/regional/hamburg/ufo/ueber-hamburg-st-pauli-29394640.bild.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bild.de">[BILD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bild.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de...</span></span></span>

No penalty was imposed. The contemporary account stated that the kite was flying at about 60 metres, below the cited urban maximum of 100 metres, and sufficiently far from Hamburg Airport’s boundary under the rules reported at the time. That detail further weakens any suggestion that the episode ended with an unexplained or illicit aerial intrusion: police located the object, its operator and its means of control, and apparently found no offence requiring action.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bild.de/regional/hamburg/ufo/ueber-hamburg-st-pauli-29394640.bild.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bild.de">[BILD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bild.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de...</span></span></span>

## How darkness concealed the kite and line

The visual puzzle was created less by the kite itself than by what witnesses could not see. During daylight, an observer might identify its fabric, outline, line and relationship to a person standing below. At night, those features could disappear against the sky while the LEDs remained conspicuous.

This removed the ordinary clues needed to judge scale and distance. A three-metre kite at 60 metres can resemble a far larger object at a much greater altitude when there are no visible reference points beside it. Official aviation guidance warns that night observation reduces depth perception and makes the distance of isolated lights difficult to judge. Visual references are normally what allow an observer to estimate distance, speed and depth; without them, even trained pilots can misinterpret illuminated objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.faasafety.gov/files/events/SO/SO15/2024/SO15134204/YourSensesInTheShadows.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: faasafety.gov">[FAASafety]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">faasafety.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Your Senses in the Shadows</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nighttime Visual Illusions...January 24, 2025 — By using visual references, the pilot can gather information about distance, speed, and...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 24, 2025</span></span></span>

The arrangement of 120 lights also helps explain why witnesses perceived structure rather than a single indistinct glow. A cluster of red, green and blue points fixed to a kite would preserve its internal pattern as the kite turned. From the ground, the lights could therefore appear to belong to one coherent object even though the supporting fabric was invisible.

Movement added another misleading cue. A large kite can climb, dip, turn, bank and change direction in gusts, while an experienced operator can alter its attitude through the line. Those movements may appear purposeful without requiring an engine or autonomous guidance. Because witnesses could not see the tether, the lights seemed free-flying. A change in the kite’s angle could also compress, widen or partially hide the LED pattern, creating the impression that an aerial craft was rotating or changing shape.

Urban surroundings did not necessarily make identification easier. Bright signs, street lighting and illuminated buildings can reduce contrast and interfere with dark adaptation, while the open area above the field provides relatively few nearby objects against which to judge an airborne display. Research on artificial night lighting likewise shows that glare and light scattered within the eye can reduce the visibility of faint background detail.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09103" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skyglow inside your eyes: intraocular scattering and artificial brightness of the night skyDecember 18, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 18, 2022</span></span></span>

The mechanism was therefore straightforward:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the LEDs remained bright while the kite’s fabric disappeared;
* the line and operator were not visible to distant observers;
* the light pattern suggested the outline of a structured object;
* wind and line control produced apparently manoeuvrable movement;
* uncertain distance made the display seem larger or higher than it was.

</div>

None of these effects requires witnesses to be careless or dishonest. They describe a difficult visual situation in which the most noticeable information—the lights—was genuine, while the information needed to interpret those lights was hidden.

## Why multiple witnesses did not prove a craft

The number of reports made the event important enough for police to investigate, but it did not make an extraordinary explanation more likely. Several people observing the same illuminated kite would naturally provide several broadly similar accounts. Their agreement establishes that a conspicuous display was present; it does not independently establish its size, distance or identity.

This distinction is central to evaluating mass UFO sightings. Witnesses are genuinely independent only in a limited sense when they are all looking at the same object under the same poor viewing conditions. They may share the same missing information: none can see the tether, none has a reliable scale reference, and all are viewing the same coloured pattern against darkness. Repetition can therefore strengthen confidence that “something was there” without strengthening the claim that it was an unconventional aircraft.

Police observation should be treated with the same care. The officers’ involvement confirms that the lights were visible and initially puzzling. It does not mean that the police endorsed an exotic interpretation. On the contrary, their continuing search produced the decisive evidence: a physical kite, a known operator and a direct explanation for the colours, movement and location.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bild.de/regional/hamburg/ufo/ueber-hamburg-st-pauli-29394640.bild.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bild.de">[BILD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bild.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO-Alarm über St. Pauli: Polizei im Dauereinsatz &#124; Regional &#124; BILD.de...</span></span></span>

The approximately two-hour delay before identification is also unsurprising. Locating the person beneath an elevated object can be harder than seeing the object itself, especially across a broad field and surrounding streets. Wind may carry a kite sideways from its operator, and the line may be almost impossible to follow at night. The delay records the practical difficulty of tracing the display, not resistance of the evidence to an ordinary explanation.

## A well-explained case with a thin public record

The available public documentation is unusually clear about the solution but narrow in its sourcing. The detailed contemporary account supplies the time, police response, dimensions, altitude, number of LEDs and the operator’s explanation. Accessible police incident logs, photographs showing the entire kite-and-line arrangement, recordings of the emergency calls and formal investigative documents have not become prominent parts of the public case record.

That limitation matters when assessing minor details. The exact number of callers is described only as dozens, and there is little published information about their viewing positions, the duration of individual observations or how they described the apparent object before learning the answer. Claims that witnesses saw a particular shape, speed or complex flight path should therefore be treated cautiously unless supported by a contemporaneous account.

The central identification, however, does not depend on reconstructing every call. Police found an illuminated kite operating in the reported area while the event was in progress. Its altitude, coloured lights and manoeuvrability matched the observations. No later reporting has produced contradictory evidence such as a second unidentified object, radar detection or sightings continuing after the kite was brought down. ## What the case adds to Hamburg’s UFO history

The St Pauli incident is useful precisely because it was resolved. It provides a rare opportunity to compare the appearance of an apparent UFO with the object that generated it, rather than leaving investigators to choose among several untestable possibilities.

It shows that a convincing urban UFO report can arise from four ordinary ingredients: a real airborne object, novel lighting, darkness and incomplete viewing geometry. It also shows why witnesses may reasonably describe an object as structured and manoeuvrable even when they cannot see the structure responsible for either quality.

Within Hamburg’s wider pattern of reports, the case belongs alongside meteors, balloons, drones, space activity and other unfamiliar lights that first prompted concern but became less mysterious after [investigation]({{ 'investigation/' | relative_url }}). The most valuable lesson is not that witnesses imagined the display. They correctly saw coloured lights moving over St Pauli. Their error was in treating those lights as a complete view of the object.

The St Pauli lights are therefore best classified as a conclusively identified UFO report: unidentified during the initial calls, verified as a real phenomenon by attending police, and then explained by direct discovery of an LED-equipped kite and its operator. Far from supporting an unknown-craft claim, the case demonstrates how quickly apparent aerial technology can emerge when an ordinary object is visible only through the lights attached to it.

      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
